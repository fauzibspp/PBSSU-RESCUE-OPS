**Decision date:** 2026-11-08

## Owner-approved policy package

The [decision register](DECISION-REGISTER.md) records the exact approved package. In summary: use an incident-level roster and individual assignments; require eligible active organization membership and at most one active incident assignment per responder; allow explicit pre-DISPATCHED release/replacement while preserving history; keep emergency breakdown and post-DISPATCHED release/cancellation deferred; require a human command plus active eligible responder and AVAILABLE vehicle for DISPATCHED; keep ON_SCENE unavailable until an approved arrival evidence standard exists; preserve G-07, server-derived actor/receipt time and human-reported append-only movement; enforce vehicle and responder uniqueness transactionally; and use only forward migrations with no invented grants or API operations.

Policy approval, scope freeze, and implementation authorization are distinct. The owner resolved duty and the Technical Lead's bounded clarification defines post-DISPATCHED assignment disposition. Preflight is blocked by the unresolved global-versus-tenant scope of responder uniqueness. Future release workflow design remains deferred.

## Blocking decision and exact action required

1. **Duty contract resolved by owner:** optional/nullable response and database field; no request field/default/enum. No duty value is generated or inferred.
2. **Post-DISPATCHED assignment disposition clarified by the Technical Lead on 2026-11-08:** personnel and vehicle assignment intervals remain active until a separately authorized future resource-release workflow explicitly ends them. Sprint 4 does not implement release, replacement, emergency breakdown, or stand-down. No automatic release occurs on any lifecycle transition. Sprint 4 does not enable ON_SCENE, MONITORING, RETURNING, COMPLETED, or CLOSED. Future COMPLETED/CLOSED transitions must remain blocked while any active assignments are unresolved; the future release workflow's authority, evidence and transaction semantics remain deferred to a separate operational-policy decision.
3. **Responder uniqueness scope remains unresolved:** the previous proposed global canonical-`users.id` interpretation is withdrawn. The owner must specify global-per-user or one-per-tenant before assignment schema and tests can be frozen.

The post-DISPATCHED assignment disposition ambiguity is resolved by the bounded Technical Lead clarification above. The table/operation inventory remains a proposal of exactly three tables/four existing operations, but the manifest is NOT FROZEN until responder uniqueness scope is resolved. Further release workflow design remains deferred and outside this scope.
